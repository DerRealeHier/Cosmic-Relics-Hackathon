How to Explore & Customize Your Cockpit

🖐️

1. 3D Rotation, Free-Flight & Zoom

Grab and drag 🖐️ left / right / up / down on the 3D scene to rotate the Moon or Mars horizon—or steer the free-flying spacecraft in Universe mode! Use your mouse wheel ↕ to zoom in close, and click any 3D model to inspect it.

👆

2. Radar Map & Quick Arrows

Click any glowing beacon pin 👆 on the top-right radar map to jump directly to that landing site or deep-space trajectory. You can also use the « Previous / Next » buttons or your keyboard’s ← / → arrow keys.

3. Move, Resize & Reset Layout

Click ✥ Customize Layout in the bottom bar to unlock all panels! Drag any window by its top ⋮⋮ handle bar to place it wherever you want, pull the bottom-right corner ↘ to make it as large or small as you like, and click ↺ Reset Default anytime to restore the standard view.
